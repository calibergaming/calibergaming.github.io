Caliber Gaming

Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026. This describes what the Caliber Gaming mobile app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version

We collect what the app needs to match you with players, centres and tournaments near you — and nothing else. There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no third-party trackers in the app. Your location is rounded to roughly 500 metres before it ever leaves your phone, so neither we nor other players ever hold your exact position.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to create your account and sign you in.
  • Your profile: gamer tag, optional name, bio and city, the games you play with your in-game names and ranks, your skill level, and whether you are looking for a squad.
  • Your approximate location, only if you grant location permission. It is rounded to about 500 metres on your device before being sent, and is used to show you nearby players, centres and events.
  • Your activity on Caliber: tournament registrations, bookings at centres and with coaches, squad membership, and match statistics.

What we do not collect

  • Your precise GPS position — it is never transmitted or stored.
  • Your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or call history.
  • Your card or bank details. Payments are handled by our payment provider, Razorpay; your card details go to them and are never seen or stored by Caliber.
  • Any advertising identifier, and any behavioural profile for advertising.

Who can see what

Other players can see your public profile — gamer tag, bio, city, games and in-game names, skill level, badges and statistics — and your approximate distance from them. Gaming centres and tournament organisers see the details of bookings and registrations you make with them. Nobody can see your email address.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts the Caliber database and handles sign-in.
  • Razorpay processes payments when you pay for an entry or a booking.
  • Expo delivers app updates. Update checks do not carry your personal data.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone for advertising.

Location permission

Location is optional. If you decline it, the app still works — you simply will not see distances or the nearby radar. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone's settings.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data at any time from the Profile tab in the app. See Delete your account for exactly what is removed and what is kept.

Age

Caliber Gaming handles payments for tournament entries and bookings and is intended for people aged 18 and over. We ask you to confirm this when you create your profile. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Keeping your data safe

Data is held in an access-controlled database where each person can only read and change their own records, enforced at the database itself rather than only in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@calibergaming.in
