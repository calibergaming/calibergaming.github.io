Delete your account

How to permanently remove your Caliber Gaming account and the data attached to it.

From inside the app

This is the fastest way, and it takes effect immediately.

  1. Open Caliber Gaming and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Type your gamer tag to confirm, then tap Delete my account.

If you can't sign in

Email support@calibergaming.in from the address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my account. We will confirm it is you and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: gamer tag, name, bio, city, in-game names, skill level and badges
  • Your approximate location
  • Your friends, friend requests, squads and squad invitations
  • Your tournament registrations and your bookings at centres and with coaches
  • Your sign-in account and email address

What is kept, and why

If you run a gaming centre, organise tournaments or coach on Caliber, those listings stay on the platform without your name or details attached. Other players have bookings, registrations and results connected to them, and deleting those records would erase other people's history too.

We may also keep records of completed payments where tax or accounting law requires it. Those records are kept separately and are not used to identify you on Caliber.

Before you can delete

If other people are currently depending on you, the app will ask you to settle up first and will tell you exactly what is outstanding. This happens when you have a tournament still running, upcoming bookings at your centre, or coaching sessions still to deliver. Finish or cancel them and deletion goes through straight away.
