What it does

Four things, all built around one question: who and what is actually near me right now?

The radar

Gamers, centres and LAN events plotted by real distance and direction. Your own position is rounded to about 500 m before it ever leaves your phone, so nobody sees where you live.

Squads

Say which games you play, your in-game name and rank, and whether you're looking. Find the four other people who want the same scrim at the same hour.

Gaming centres

See which centres near you have PCs or consoles free, what they charge an hour, and their opening times. Book the seat in the app and pay at the counter.

Tournaments

BGMI, Valorant, Free Fire, CS2 and more — online cups and LAN events near you, with slots, rules, check-in and results. Organisers run theirs from the same app.

Getting started

About two minutes, and you never need to give a phone number.

  1. Pick a gamer tagSign in with an email, choose a tag and your city.
  2. Add your gamesYour titles, in-game names and ranks, so squads can actually find you.
  3. Turn on the radarAllow approximate location and see what's within 5 km. Decline it and everything else still works — you just won't see distances.

Run a gaming centre or organise events?

Installing it the first time

Caliber isn't on the Play Store yet, so Android will ask you a couple of questions it doesn't ask for Play apps. That's normal for any app installed directly — here is exactly what you will see.

  1. Tap DownloadYour browser may ask whether to keep the file. Keep it.
  2. Open the downloaded fileAndroid will say your browser isn't allowed to install apps yet. Tap Settings, turn on “Allow from this source”, then come back.
  3. If Play Protect warns youYou may see “unsafe app blocked” or an offer to scan. Caliber isn't on Play yet, which is the only reason it shows — tap More details, then Install anyway.
  4. Open Caliber and sign inEmail and a gamer tag. No phone number, ever.

Updates after that arrive on their own — you won't need to download the file again every time.

Caliber is new and growing city by city, starting with Dehradun. If the radar looks quiet where you are, it's because you're early — bring the people you already play with and it fills up fast.
